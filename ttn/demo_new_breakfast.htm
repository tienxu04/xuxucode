Breakfast @ Tuoi Tre News – October 8

3:52

Good morning from Vietnam!

Catch up on the news in Vietnam today:

Tragic Violent Attack Investigated in Ca Mau

The Ca Mau Department of Public Security on Wednesday initiated a criminal case against 40-year-old Tran Chi Cong, a resident of Hamlet 3, Nam Can Commune, Ca Mau Province, for allegedly killing a six-year-old girl and seriously injuring her mother, a 39-year-old elementary school teacher, and her 11-year-old sister in a knife attack at their home on Monday.

HCMC Police Issue Advisory on 122 Flood-Prone Areas

The Traffic Police Division under the Ho Chi Minh City Department of Public Security on Wednesday issued a list of 122 flood-prone locations across Ho Chi Minh City, including 83 areas vulnerable to heavy rain and poor drainage and 39 affected by high tides, and advised residents to monitor weather, tide and traffic updates and avoid flooded roads.

Hanoi Inaugurates VND600 Billion Senior Healthcare Complex

Hanoi authorities and the Hanoi Housing Development and Investment Corporation (HANDICO), along with several partners, on Wednesday inaugurated the HANDICO Hanoi healthcare complex at 5 Thanh Cong Street in Giang Vo Ward, combining medical care, elderly residential care, and rehabilitation in a VND600 billion (US$23 million) facility designed as a model to ‘bring the hospital home’ for older people.

Downpour Causes Traffic Chaos and Flooding in HCMC

Torrential rain during rush hour on Wednesday evening caused severe flooding and serious traffic congestion on Pham Van Dong Boulevard, Nguyen Thai Son Overpass, Phan Van Tri and Le Quang Dinh Streets, and the Go Vap Six-Way Intersection in Ho Chi Minh City, with many vehicles stalled and traffic descending into chaos, according to Tuoi Tre (Youth) online newspaper's observations.

Vietnam and U.S. Nearing Final Reciprocal Trade Deal

Vietnam Considers National Strategic Power Generation Corporation

Bui Quoc Hung, deputy director of the Electricity Department under the Vietnamese Ministry of Industry and Trade, said on Wednesday that the ministry was considering establishing a national strategic power generation corporation by merging strategic multi-purpose hydropower plants to help fund investment in strategically important power projects.

HCMC Police FC Draw Opener in ASEAN Club Championship

Ho Chi Minh City Police FC were held to a 1-1 draw by Thailand's Ratchaburi FC on Wednesday night after conceding an equalizer in stoppage time in their Group A opener at the 2026-27 ASEAN Club Championship (Shopee Cup) in Thailand.
